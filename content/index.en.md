Brillio, in partnership with Payment Components, delivers a cloud-native payment modernization hub powered by aplonHUB. This robust solution enables financial institutions to standardize and modernize their payment infrastructure efficiently, across multiple schemes and instruments.

**Core Capabilities**
**Multi-Scheme Support:** SWIFT MT, ISO 20022, SEPA, FedNow

**Multi-Instrument Processing:** Credit Transfers, Direct Debits, Instant Payments

**AI-Powered Compliance:** Real-time fraud detection and AML screening

**Seamless Core Integration:** API, file, and queue-based integration with core banking and AML systems

**Advanced Message Processing:** Parsing, validation, transformation, automatic replies

**Cloud-Native Architecture:** Deployed on AWS EKS for scalability, resilience, and operational agility

**Robust Security:** Role-based access control, audit trails, and secure APIs

In this workshop, we will:

- Demonstrate how to initiate, track, and manage cross-border payments using aplonHUB

- Showcase end-to-end flows including message validation, routing, compliance, and settlement

- Explore opportunities for real-time visibility and operational efficiency across jurisdictions
